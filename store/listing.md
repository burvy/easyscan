## Full description

Turn homework pages into one tidy PDF. EasyScan uses your phone's camera to capture paper, straighten page edges and put multiple pages together—no separate scanner needed.

SCAN AND EDIT YOUR WAY

• Capture one page or keep going to build a multi-page scan. EasyScan detects page edges and suggests a perspective-corrected crop; adjust the corners yourself when needed.
• Choose Original, Grayscale, Enhance or Black & White. Enhance brightens uneven or shadowed paper while keeping coloured ink. Black & White can remove pale blue notebook ruling while keeping pencil and pen marks legible.
• Add marks with a coloured pen or a paper-matching white-out brush. Undo and redo your strokes.
• Reorder pages, retake a photo or delete a page before exporting.

EXPORT AND PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF

• Export your pages together as one PDF. Choose Fit to photo, A4 or US Letter, and Smaller file or Standard quality.
• Reopen, rename and manage past scans right on your phone.
• Use Android's share sheet to choose where to send an exported PDF.

No account is needed. Your scans and edits are stored in EasyScan's private storage on your device. Scanning and editing work offline; an internet connection is used for ads and consent. EasyScan is ad-supported.

EasyScan is intended for people aged 13 and over. For details about scan storage, ads and your choices, read our Privacy Policy: https://burvy.github.io/easyscan/privacy-policy/
