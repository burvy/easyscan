**Effective date: October 28, 2026**

EasyScan is a document-scanning app published by Burvy. This policy explains what happens to information when you use EasyScan on Android.

## Scans stay on your device

EasyScan uses the camera only when you choose to take or retake a scan. Android asks for Camera permission for that feature. EasyScan does not request access to your photo library, microphone, contacts, or device location.

Your captured photos, scan edits, page thumbnails, scan names, and exported PDFs are processed and stored in EasyScan's private storage on your device. EasyScan does not upload your scans or PDFs to a server. You can delete pages or whole scans from the app. Exported PDFs are made in temporary storage; when you choose **Export**, Android's share sheet lets you send the PDF to an app or destination you select. That recipient's own privacy practices then apply. EasyScan does not receive a copy through that sharing action.

Your scans remain on the device until you delete them or remove EasyScan. Depending on your Android version and device backup settings, some app-private data may also be included in Android backups; those backups are managed through your device and Google account settings.

## Ads and Google's services

EasyScan uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request and manage ad consent where required. The app may contact Google for consent information and to load ads. Ads are not part of scanning: the scanner works offline, but ads and consent updates need an internet connection.

Google says the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

- **IP address**, which Google may use to estimate your general (approximate) location. EasyScan does not request precise location permission.
- **App interactions**, such as app launches, taps, and video-ad views.
- **Diagnostics**, such as app launch time, hangs, and energy use.
- **Device or account identifiers**, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google states that this SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google's collection and use of this information are governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [information about Google's advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

Where applicable, UMP presents Google's consent choices before ads are requested. You can revisit consent or privacy choices through Google's **Privacy options** control in EasyScan when it is shown. You can also manage or reset the advertising ID in Android settings. The choices and controls available depend on your location and device.

EasyScan does not use your scans to select ads, and its developer does not receive the advertising information described above from the SDK.

## Children's privacy

EasyScan is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not designed or directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and consent services are provided by Google and operate as described above. If you believe a child has contacted the developer or provided information directly, please email us.

## Your choices and deletion

You can delete individual pages or scans inside EasyScan. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to any Android backup described above. EasyScan has no user accounts or scan-upload service, and the developer does not hold your scans on a server. The developer therefore has no server-side scan copy to delete on request. Google handles data collected by its advertising and consent services under Google's own policies and controls.

## Changes to this policy

If EasyScan's data practices change, this policy will be updated. The effective date at the top indicates when it was last revised.
